# Brisbane automation roles and skills

Research snapshot: 18 October 2026.

The strongest project direction in this sample is a business workflow that solves a clear operational problem, connects systems, handles exceptions and gives staff reliable evidence of what happened. AI is a significant part of the software roles, but the ads also expect integration, testing, communication and ongoing support.

## Sample and limits

Reviewed the full descriptions of the first **50 distinct advertisement IDs** encountered on [SEEK page 1](https://au.seek.com/automation-jobs/in-All-Brisbane-QLD) and [page 2](https://au.seek.com/automation-jobs/in-All-Brisbane-QLD?page=2), preserving display order and counting promoted ads once. “Top” means search display order, not highest salary, best employer or strongest suitability.

Repeated featured cards for Trisco, Australian Country Choice, IRIS and LETO were removed after confirming their job IDs. Ads 4 and 14 have different IDs but near-identical text and may represent the same recruitment campaign. The sample therefore measures ads, not 50 independent vacancies.

The Brisbane search includes location exceptions: ad 35 is for Boggabri, NSW; ad 28 requires visits to Thornton. Ad 41 starts in February 2027. Search order and availability change; this is a dated snapshot rather than a complete Brisbane market census.

Each ad has one primary family below. Hybrid roles could reasonably fit more than one family. “Industrial and hardware” includes engineering project delivery, laboratory testing and support for mining technology.

## What employers need

**Understand and improve an existing process.** Trisco asks for process mapping, requirements, vendor coordination and adoption. Flash Co needs contractor scheduling, evidence from the field, accounting integration and training. P&C describes approximately 70% of the role as maintaining and improving existing systems. Discovery, practical scope and handover deserve as much attention as building a new interface. [Trisco](https://au.seek.com/job/95234381), [Flash Co](https://au.seek.com/job/94991636), [P&C](https://au.seek.com/job/95234070).

**Connect applications and manage data.** APIs/webhooks appear in 8 of the 13 relevant ads. Examples include CRM, accounting, dashboards and enterprise applications. Language skills support this work: JavaScript/TypeScript appears in five relevant ads, Python in four, and SQL/PostgreSQL in three. Integral specifically wants repeatable synthetic data, masking and database resets. [Adpen](https://au.seek.com/job/94897023), [Integral](https://au.seek.com/job/94755423).

**Prove quality and support operation.** All five conventional software QA ads name Playwright; their requirements also cover frameworks, API tests, CI/CD, failure diagnosis, performance, test data or release gates. Task Recruitment includes authorisation and error cases. Michael Page wants measurable coverage and stable regression suites. Reliability is also central to the business and operations roles. [Task Recruitment](https://au.seek.com/job/95298722), [Michael Page](https://au.seek.com/job/95220100).

**Use AI at the level the role requires.** Ten of the 13 relevant ads mention AI, but the expectations differ. Best Practice explicitly asks for daily AI use in coding/test generation. P&C values AI/Copilot exposure while prioritising existing workflows. Adpen requires previously deployed agents and integrations. Virgin asks for controlled generative AI alongside UiPath, AWS and enterprise engineering. Blackbook offers a supported associate pathway across AI, data, digital and automation. Agentic development experience helps explain how a project was built; demonstrating a product that runs AI is separate evidence. [Best Practice](https://au.seek.com/job/95274572), [P&C](https://au.seek.com/job/95234070), [Adpen](https://au.seek.com/job/94897023), [Virgin](https://au.seek.com/job/94936072), [Blackbook](https://au.seek.com/job/95260505).

**Communicate, train and take ownership.** The relevant ads consistently connect technical delivery with stakeholders, requirements, documentation, training, mentoring or support. A working demonstration needs a business explanation: who uses it, what starts it, which decisions remain human and how success is checked.

## Experience and access requirements

Much of the software sample is experienced hiring: Task Recruitment asks for 4+ years; Auto & General’s senior role asks for 5+; Best Practice asks for 7+ years QA and 4+ automation; Michael Page asks for 8+ automation and 3+ senior/lead; Datacom asks for 15+ years in large-scale operations. Blackbook is the clearest software associate pathway in this sample and starts in February 2027.

Other gates matter independently of a portfolio: Datacom specifies Australian citizenship and NV1 eligibility; Task specifies Australian/NZ citizenship or Australian permanent residency and five days in the office. Several electrical posts require a current electrical licence. A project can demonstrate skills; these experience and eligibility requirements remain separate.
